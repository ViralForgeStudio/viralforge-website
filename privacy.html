Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

ViralForge Studio ("ViralForge", "we", "us", or "our") provides creator tools for generating, reviewing, and publishing short-form video content. This Privacy Policy explains what information we receive, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use the ViralForge Studio website and creator tools.

Information we receive

Depending on the features you use, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, including video files, captions, text, audio, images, and other content that you choose to process through ViralForge.

When you connect a TikTok account, we may receive basic account information made available through TikTok Login Kit, such as your TikTok display name and account identifier, together with the authorization information required to provide the TikTok features you request.

We may also receive technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as browser information, session information, request information, and information necessary to diagnose technical problems.

TikTok and third-party services

If you choose to connect TikTok, ViralForge uses TikTok's authentication and content publishing services to authenticate your account and, with your authorization, provide TikTok publishing features.

ViralForge only accesses TikTok information and performs TikTok actions that are authorized by you and supported by the permissions granted to the application.

TikTok is an independent third-party service. Your use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own terms, privacy policies, and technical requirements.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate ViralForge Studio;
  • authenticate users and maintain secure sessions;
  • process creator-requested video and other content;
  • provide TikTok publishing features that you explicitly request;
  • maintain and secure the service;
  • diagnose and resolve technical problems; and
  • respond to support, privacy, or data-related requests.

We do not sell personal information.

Content you provide

Content that you upload or otherwise provide to ViralForge remains under your control. We process that content only as necessary to provide the features you request and to operate and secure the service.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights and permissions necessary to use and publish any content you provide.

Cookies and session information

ViralForge uses essential browser cookies or similar session mechanisms when necessary to authenticate users, maintain secure sessions, and provide requested features.

These mechanisms may be used to maintain your authenticated TikTok session while you use the publishing features of the service.

Service providers and infrastructure

ViralForge may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate the website, securely store application data or media, maintain authentication sessions, and provide the requested functionality.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services to ViralForge and are subject to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Authentication credentials and tokens used to provide authorized third-party functionality are handled using server-side mechanisms designed to limit unauthorized access.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

Your choices and data requests

You may stop using ViralForge at any time. You may also request information about personal data associated with your use of the service and, where applicable, request correction or deletion of that information.

If you no longer want ViralForge to access your TikTok account, you may revoke the authorization through the applicable TikTok account controls.

Children

ViralForge Studio is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the applicable services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
